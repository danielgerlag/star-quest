STAR QUEST: THE BURDEN OF COMMAND
Original alternate-timeline walkthrough

SPOILER WARNING
This file reveals every required puzzle, all four irreversible decisions, and
all three outcomes. The staged objective hints in the game are less explicit.

This is an original, unofficial Star Trek: The Next Generation fan adventure.
The premise diverges after Picard's capture. It does not recreate an episode
script. Riker is the sole playable captain; specialists perform their own jobs.

HOW TO READ THESE INSTRUCTIONS
Look, Talk, Scan, and Use mean the four interface verbs. "Apply" means select
the named inventory item and click the named target. Travel between this
chapter's rooms with the map. Read dialogue and select its named choice when
instructed. No hidden timer runs while you investigate. Looking and talking
out of order cannot strand the game; revisit a specialist after new evidence.
Inventory acquisitions are repeatable and no critical item is consumed.

CHAPTER 1: THE EMPTY CHAIR

1. Main Bridge: Look at Chair. Talk to Worf, then Use Command seal. This
   establishes witnessed local command without treating a remote message as
   the ship's captain. Look at Damage board: its burned seam and repeated
   all-clear are evidence that the picture is stale.
2. Primary Power Gallery: Scan Bypass and Talk to Geordi. Apply Command PADD
   to Repair desk. The bridge's old snapshot and the real bypass measurement
   agree once their times are aligned. Geordi handles the actual repair.
3. Marigold Docking Vestibule: Look at Manifest to obtain it. Scan Crate.
   The crate contains power-responsive filaments; do not connect it. Talking
   to Vale and Troi gives useful context but is not a substitute for a scan.
4. Sickbay Intake: Look at Birth record and Talk to Crusher. Apply Command
   PADD to Records. Alternatively, apply the Manifest or Tender medical report
   after obtaining both records and observing the wristband. Twelve people
   departed and a child was born: thirteen arrived. This explains the life
   signs, not the suspicious cargo.
5. Look at Bed board. Talk to Crusher again after comparing the records and
   scanning the crate. She authorizes separate cargo containment and humane
   passenger screening. Return to the tender and Use Crate.
6. Main Bridge: Talk to Shelby. Choose:
   - Bring passengers aboard: occupy the isolation annex, keep the shuttle.
   - Keep the tender under shuttle escort: keep the annex, commit the shuttle.
   Neither option abandons the passengers. The choice cannot be reversed
   after it has been recorded. Use Chair to proceed.

CHAPTER 2: BORROWED AUTHORITY

1. Captain's Ready Room: Look at Dispatch cylinder and Scan Stamp reader.
   Physical receipt is 03:19, although the order claims an issue time of 03:23.
2. Emergency Relay Alcove: Look at Antenna feed. It is physically unplugged.
   Scan Thermal logger: disconnection occurred at 03:11 on an independent
   clock. Talk to Geordi, then Use Optical kit to obtain the reusable isolator.
3. Computer Archive Annex: Talk to Data. Apply Dispatch cylinder to Signature
   reader. The signer is real, but the transport header was regenerated here.
   Apply Command PADD to Route comparator. The three records establish a
   replay from the local emergency cache, not a new external instruction.
   You receive Origin proof that other captains can reproduce themselves.
4. Observation Lounge: Apply Origin proof to Traffic model. This is required:
   it explains the practical tradeoff before you choose a relay disposition.
5. Ready Room: Talk to Shelby. Choose:
   - Cut: remove the suspect carrier and its noise, but lose automatic
     navigation. Chapter 3 requires three visual bearings.
   - Supervised: retain a one-way reader and navigation samples, but perform
     active noise rejection. You receive a Supervised noise profile.
   Use Command desk to proceed. The later warning transmitter is separate:
   cutting the incoming relay does not prevent narrow-beam rescue traffic.

CHAPTER 3: THE SCATTERED FLEET

1. Stellar Cartography: Talk to Data. Follow the route actually chosen:
   CUT RELAY:
     Look at Split star. Scan Dead buoy. Travel to Rescue Signal Array,
     Look at Mast crescent, then Use Sight shutter. Three independent
     measurements establish fleet positions with honest uncertainty margins.
   SUPERVISED RELAY:
     Travel to Rescue Signal Array. Scan Carrier reader for the current
     interference, then apply Supervised noise profile to that reader.
     The stored profile alone is not enough: the noise has shifted.
2. Salvage Receiving Hold: Look at Salvage pallet, Scan Load bench, and Talk
   to Geordi. Use Release console. You receive a Certified power cell and
   a service allocation. The crushed weapons scrap is not needed.
3. Rescue Signal Array: Apply Origin proof to Carrier reader. With verified
   fleet positions and real support to offer, the warning can be sent to the
   independent captains. This works with either relay choice.
4. Aft Evacuation Bay: Look at Frosted hatch and Talk to Crusher. Apply
   Certified power cell to Beacon sockets. Technicians warm the iced contacts
   safely and recharge the cell afterward; it remains available.
5. Apply Command PADD to Lane board. Patient acceleration limits, fleet
   uncertainty margins, and live beacon tests define a protected lane.
6. Stellar Cartography: Use Rendezvous. The four captains offer limited,
   specific aid. You obtain Fleet support agreement and proceed.

CHAPTER 4: A HUMAN SIGNAL

1. Long-Range Sensor Gallery: Scan Respiratory trace, Look at Repair shutters,
   and Talk to Data. Apply Fleet support agreement to Parallax desk. The
   independent observer resolves one living body inside a maintenance cavity.
   This is a rescue location, not a command channel to the Collective.
2. Medical Logistics Annex: Look at Annex beds and Talk to Crusher. She
   provides Medical isolation kit and the practical receiving protocol.
   Follow the earlier tender commitment:
   PASSENGERS ABOARD:
     Look at Partition plan. Apply Command PADD to Medical dispatch. Wayfarer
     supplies nurses for a proper overflow ward; the passengers are not
     simply removed from their beds to make room for Picard.
   TENDER ESCORTED:
     Look at Medical dispatch to verify the shuttle is still committed.
     Apply Fleet support agreement there. Morrow explicitly assumes the
     independent receiving-buffer duty; the shuttle keeps its promise.
3. Isolated Transport Room: Scan Diagnostic lead and Talk to O'Brien. Apply
   Optical isolator to Diagnostic lead. Apply Medical isolation kit to
   Receiving pad. Look at Dwell dial, then Use it to arm the bounded transport.
   Arming does not start a hidden countdown.
4. Quiet Isolation Room: Look at Patient monitor and Talk to Troi. If this
   is your first conversation, Talk to her again to open the decision:
   MEDICAL:
     Protect Picard's clinical baseline. He has the better chance of coherent
     recovery. A later precision attack needs a fleet probe and reserve cover.
   TACTICAL:
     Take one bounded sample of local repair timing. It reduces the later
     reconnaissance burden, but Picard suffers injured memory and uncertain
     long-term recovery. It does not yield universal Borg controls.
   Every final strategy remains available under either choice.
5. Transport Room: Use Receiving pad to authorize the chosen extraction.
6. Isolation Room: Talk to Picard. His response differs materially according
   to the contact protocol. No tactical interrogation is required.
7. Sensor Gallery: Use Parallax desk to proceed.

CHAPTER 5: ANOTHER WAY TO FIGHT

All three plans are tested before you choose. Reservations are conditional:
testing alternatives does not spend all three sets of resources.

1. Materials Analysis Laboratory: Scan Armor sample and Talk to Data.
   Apply Origin proof to Control model to reject the false universal-control
   hypothesis. Authentic instructions cannot command unknown physical systems.
2. At Seam bench:
   - With medical contact, apply Fleet support agreement. The exterior probe
     and reserve shielding are explicitly pledged if Precision is selected.
   - With tactical contact, apply Limited tactical sample.
   A single moving seam can be exploited once; repeated identical fire fails.
3. Auxiliary Planning Bridge: Talk to Worf for local-clock firing doctrine.
4. Fleet Liaison Wardroom: Look at Formation chart. Apply Fleet support
   agreement to Firing model. The two-pass displacement rehearsal succeeds
   without inventing a third pass for the damaged Sable. Talk to Crusher for
   the dependent evacuation protocol.
5. Separation Service Hangar: Look at Tow model and Talk to Geordi. Apply
   Certified power cell to Tow model. The physical rehearsal demonstrates a
   guided, empty engineering hull blocking a turn. Fleet fire must still force
   that turn and destroy the exposed drive structure. No living pilot stays.
6. Scan Headcount board. Apply Marigold manifest to Evacuation desk. The birth,
   live count, and receiving teams reconcile. Earlier medical and escort
   commitments stay in force. Dependents reach the protected fleet refuge.
7. Planning Bridge: Look at Asset board, then apply Command PADD there.
   Talk to Worf again for the cross-specialist rehearsal and abort conditions.
8. Use Decision console. Choose one tested strategy:
   - Precision: best chance to retain the Enterprise. Spend observer cover,
     plus the pledged probe and reserve shielding after medical contact.
   - Fleet: robust staggered displacement fire. Expose damaged allies and
     commit Enterprise's propulsion margin. Expect casualties and tows.
   - Sacrifice: evacuate and separate the engineering hull. Save the crew
     aboard the saucer, but lose Enterprise as a functioning starship.
   This choice is irreversible. Use Decision console again to proceed.

CHAPTER 6: THE BURDEN OF COMMAND

The three plans require different physical execution, not a single ending
button. Follow your selected plan, then complete the common confirmation.

PRECISION PATH
1. Final Power Control: Look at Power grid and Talk to Data. Apply Command
   PADD to Phase bench. Medical contact spends the pledged unmanned probe and
   shield cover; tactical contact uses its limited map with a fresh passive
   check. Neither substitutes a successful old rehearsal for a current reading.
2. Independent Signal Control: Scan Lane aperture, Talk to O'Brien, then apply
   Fleet support agreement to Aiming station. Two independent observers
   confirm the phase solution and the civilian exclusion boundary.
3. Final Approach Bridge: Look at Engagement plot and Talk to Worf. Use
   Command control. The staggered seam strike breaks the drive trunk and the
   fleet finishes disabled sections outside the inhabited lanes.

FLEET PATH
1. Independent Signal Control: Scan Lane aperture and Look at Pass assignments.
   Talk to O'Brien, then apply Fleet support agreement to Pass assignments.
   Captains acknowledge local-clock passes and their own abort conditions.
2. Final Power Control: Look at Power grid and Talk to Geordi. Apply Certified
   power cell to Power grid. The cell validates the transition; ship reserves
   actually power the attack. Medical power remains protected.
3. Final Approach Bridge: Look at Engagement plot and Talk to Worf. Use
   Command control. The two-pass displacement attack breaks the approach.
   Enterprise loses propulsion and Sable is battered; no third pass is ordered.

SACRIFICE PATH
1. Casualty Coordination Room: Look at Emergency lockers and Talk to Crusher.
   Apply Marigold manifest to Casualty monitor. The live crew roster and
   independent scan account for every repair worker and transport operator.
   The engineering hull is empty before separation is authorized.
2. Final Power Control: Scan Separation clutch and Talk to Geordi. Apply
   Command PADD to Separation clutch. Geordi performs controlled separation;
   Riker commands from the saucer reserve bridge, not the sacrificed hull.
3. Independent Signal Control: Look at Lane aperture to observe the wake,
   then Scan it to verify the civilian boundary. Talk to O'Brien and apply
   Fleet support agreement to Aiming station. The guidance line has an
   independent solution and rejects unverified replacement commands.
4. Final Approach Bridge: Look at Engagement plot and Talk to Worf. Use
   Command control. Fleet fire forces a turn, the empty hull jams the wake,
   and the fleet strikes the exposed drive lattice. No crew member is left
   behind as a guidance component.

COMMON FINAL CONFIRMATION
1. Casualty Coordination Room: Scan Casualty monitor after execution. Talk to
   Crusher. Apply Command PADD to Earth channel. Surface stations and orbital
   observers verify safety independently of your targeting display.
2. Final Approach Bridge: Use Command control again to close the combat log.
   Before confirmation, repeated Use does not fire a second attack.

OUTCOMES
Recovery / A Future Still Ours:
  Precision plus medical contact. Earth is saved, Enterprise can return under
  reduced propulsion, Earth's orbital services remain intact, the fleet
  retains rescue capacity, and Picard has begun coherent recovery. The
  external probe and reserve cover were not free.
Costly Victory / The Names We Bring Home:
  Precision plus tactical contact: Earth and more ships are protected, but
  Picard's memory injury is a lasting, uncertain cost.
  Any Fleet strategy: Earth is saved, wounded crews need continuing care,
  Enterprise and Sable require tows, and two uncrewed Earth defense platforms
  need replacement. Civilian habitats and the surface are intact. Picard's
  state follows the prior contact decision rather than being reset by battle.
Last Command / The Last Command of the Enterprise:
  Any Sacrifice strategy. Earth is saved and the accounted-for crew survives,
  but Enterprise is no longer a functioning starship. Picard's care continues
  with the actual consequences of the chosen contact protocol. Earth's
  habitats and defense platforms survive; two outer approach corridors close
  for an extended wreckage clearance operation.

The tender's care, the relay procedure, Picard's condition, the ship's fate,
and the fleet's capacity remain distinct recorded outcomes. There is no
unavoidable bad-ending trap and no secret universal Borg kill switch.

REPRODUCIBLE TEST FIXTURE
src/campaign/walkthrough.ts exports walkthrough(options?), returning only
normal travel, interact, inventory-item, and dialogue-choice actions.
Options are evacuation=aboard|escort, relay=cut|supervised,
contact=medical|tactical, strategy=precision|fleet|sacrifice.
All 2 x 2 x 2 x 3 combinations are playable from a fresh game. The default is
aboard / cut / medical / precision. The fixture neither sets facts directly
nor bypasses the user interface's action contract.
